Flowen · iOS App

A calm timer for
the longest night.

Flowen times contractions with one tap and tells you when the pattern changes — so during labor you can focus on each other, not on a stopwatch and mental math. Everything stays on your phone.

What it does

Built for one night.
Designed to disappear.

⏱

One-tap timing

Tap when a contraction starts, tap when it ends. Flowen keeps timing even if you close the app or your phone locks — no lost contractions at 3 a.m.

💗

Intensity, without typing

After each contraction, rate how strong it felt on a simple 1–5 scale. That single extra signal makes the pattern picture far more useful than times alone.

📈

Pattern insight

Flowen watches duration, frequency, and intensity together and describes the rhythm in plain words — whether contractions still look like early labor or are settling into an active-labor pattern, based on published labor-progression research.

📤

Share with your midwife

Export a clean text summary or CSV of your log in one tap — exactly what the maternity ward asks for when you call, without scrolling back through hours of entries.

How it works

Three taps, start to finish.

01

Log contractions as they come

One big button. Start, stop, rate the intensity. Haptics confirm every action so you can use it without looking at the screen.

02

Watch the trend, not the clock

The trends view charts interval, duration, and intensity over time and summarizes the rhythm in plain language, so you're not doing frequency math between contractions.

03

Call with the facts in hand

When it's time to call the midwife or hospital, share the summary. They see timing, spacing, and intensity at a glance and can tell you when to come in.

Flowen is not a medical device

Its pattern summaries and estimates are informational, based on general labor patterns — not clinical monitoring or diagnosis. Always follow the guidance of your midwife, doctor, or hospital. If anything concerns you during labor, contact your maternity ward or emergency services immediately — don't wait for a pattern in an app.

Everything you log stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no analytics. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
