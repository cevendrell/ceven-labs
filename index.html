Different problems.
Same philosophy.

Every tool we build starts with peer-reviewed research on how the body works, strips away everything irrelevant, and delivers something you can act on immediately, in plain language on the device in your pocket.

🌙

Circadian science

LayOvr models your internal clock using the two-process sleep framework and Phase Response Curves, the same science chronobiology researchers use, turned into a clear day-by-day travel plan.

🤍

Labor pattern research

Flowen reads contraction timing, spacing, and intensity against published labor-progression research and describes the rhythm in plain words — so the 3 a.m. call to the midwife starts with facts, not guesses.

🚴

Biomechanical fitting

Bike Fit applies validated fitting methodologies that bike fitters use in studios: LeMond saddle height and body proportion geometry, but instantly, in your browser, from your measurements alone.

🔒

Private by default

No backend. No analytics. No accounts. Your health data and body measurements stay on your device. That's not a cost decision. It's a principle every tool is built around from the start.
